Battery capacity · %

Battery net current · A

Positive current flows into the battery; negative current indicates net discharge. The input limit is not actual charging current. Sensor readings may briefly differ.

Loading history…

Current values refresh every 20 seconds; samples are saved every minute and charts refresh every five minutes. System history is kept for 24 hours and battery history for seven days. Refresh pauses in background tabs.

24-hour charts show four-minute averages; raw minute samples remain stored. Peaks are sampled peaks, not instantaneous spikes. Traffic and I/O totals count continuous intervals only, excluding reboots and counter resets. Gaps are not joined.

Reads system counters only. No speed tests, IP addresses, browsing history, process lists or user content are collected. Charging and drivers are never controlled. Charts render in your browser.
